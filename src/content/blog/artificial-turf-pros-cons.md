[Artificial turf](/services/artificial-turf) has become one of the most requested services at Visionable Landscaping. Here's an honest breakdown to help you decide.

## The Pros

### Water Savings
A 1,000 sq ft natural lawn uses approximately 44,000 gallons of water per year in the Bay Area. Artificial turf uses zero.

### Zero Maintenance
No mowing, no fertilizing, no aerating, no reseeding. Your weekends are yours again.

### Always Green
Rain or drought, your lawn looks perfect 365 days a year. No brown patches, no seasonal dormancy.

### Durability
Modern artificial turf lasts 15-20 years with proper installation and minimal care.

### Pet Friendly
High-quality turf with proper [drainage](/services/irrigation-drainage) handles pet use beautifully. It's easy to clean and doesn't develop bare spots from traffic.

## The Cons

### Upfront Cost
Quality installation runs $12-18 per sq ft in the Bay Area, including base prep, turf, and infill. A 500 sq ft area costs $6,000-9,000.

### Heat Retention
Artificial turf can get hot in direct sun — up to 20°F warmer than natural grass. Solution: choose lighter colors and install in partially shaded areas.

### Environmental Debate
While turf saves water, it's a petroleum-based product. We use recyclable turf options when available.

### Not Suitable Everywhere
Turf works best for lawns, play areas, and pet runs. For garden beds and slopes, natural plantings are still the better choice.

## Our Installation Process

1. **Remove existing lawn** and excavate 3-4 inches
2. **Install crushed rock base** and compact thoroughly
3. **Lay weed barrier** fabric
4. **Roll out and seam** premium turf
5. **Add infill** (sand or organic) for weight and natural feel
6. **Brush and groom** for a finished look

> We only use turf with a 15+ year warranty and UV protection rated for California sun.

## Bottom Line

If you're spending $200+/month on lawn care and water, artificial turf pays for itself in 3-5 years. It's the single best investment for busy Bay Area homeowners who want a beautiful yard without the work.
