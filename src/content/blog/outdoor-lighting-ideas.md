Most homeowners think about their landscape in daylight. But the right [outdoor lighting](/services/outdoor-lighting) design can transform your yard into an entirely different experience after sunset.

## 1. Path Lighting

Guide guests safely while creating a welcoming ambiance. Low-profile LED path lights spaced 6-8 feet apart provide both function and beauty.

## 2. Uplighting Trees

Position lights at the base of specimen trees to create dramatic shadows and highlight canopy structure. This single technique can make your yard feel like a resort.

![Curved concrete patio with a lit low retaining wall and gravel border](/photos/services/lighting02.webp)

## 3. Moonlighting

Mount fixtures high in trees angled downward to simulate natural moonlight. This creates soft, dappled light that's both romantic and functional.

## 4. Step & Wall Lights

Recessed lights in steps, walls, and seat walls add safety and a modern, architectural feel to [hardscaping](/services/hardscaping) and [retaining walls](/services/retaining-walls).

## 5. String Lights

Don't underestimate the power of string lights over a dining area or patio. They create instant atmosphere at a fraction of the cost of permanent fixtures.

## 6. Water Feature Lighting

Submersible LED lights in fountains, ponds, or waterfalls create a mesmerizing focal point that draws the eye and adds movement to your nightscape.

## 7. Smart Controls

Modern landscape lighting systems connect to your phone. Set schedules, adjust brightness, and create scenes for different occasions — all from your couch.

> The best lighting design is invisible during the day and magical at night.

## Cost Expectations

- **Basic package** (paths + entry): $2,000-5,000
- **Mid-range** (paths + trees + patio): $5,000-12,000
- **Premium** (full property): $12,000-25,000+

All include LED fixtures, transformer, wiring, and professional design.
