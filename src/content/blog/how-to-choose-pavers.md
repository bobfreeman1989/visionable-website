Choosing [pavers](/services/paver-installation) is one of the most impactful decisions in any hardscape project. The right material sets the tone for your entire outdoor space — and the wrong one can mean costly replacements down the road.

## The Big Three: Concrete, Natural Stone, Porcelain

### Concrete Pavers

The workhorse of Bay Area [hardscaping](/services/hardscaping). Concrete pavers offer the best balance of durability, versatility, and value.

- **Cost**: $8-15 per sq ft installed
- **Durability**: 25-30+ years
- **Styles**: Hundreds of colors, shapes, and textures
- **Best for**: Driveways, patios, pool decks

### Natural Stone

Nothing beats the look of real stone. Flagstone, travertine, and bluestone bring an organic elegance that's hard to replicate.

- **Cost**: $15-30 per sq ft installed
- **Durability**: 50+ years
- **Styles**: Each piece is unique
- **Best for**: Garden paths, accent areas, luxury patios

### Porcelain Pavers

The newcomer that's taking Silicon Valley by storm. Porcelain offers a sleek, modern aesthetic with virtually zero maintenance.

- **Cost**: $12-25 per sq ft installed
- **Durability**: 30+ years
- **Styles**: Wood-look, stone-look, concrete-look
- **Best for**: Modern designs, pool surrounds, rooftop decks

## Climate Considerations for the Bay Area

Silicon Valley's mild climate is actually ideal for most paver types, but there are a few things to keep in mind:

> Always choose pavers rated for freeze-thaw resistance if your property is in the hills above 1,000 ft elevation.

1. **Sun exposure**: Dark pavers absorb more heat — consider lighter colors for barefoot-friendly pool areas
2. **Drainage**: Permeable pavers help with water management and may qualify for rebates
3. **Soil type**: Bay Area's expansive clay soils require proper base preparation

## Our Recommendation

For most Bay Area homeowners, **concrete pavers** offer the best value. For a premium look, pair concrete with **natural stone accents** at entry points and focal areas.
